# Open Local Dictation v0.1.0

Released 2026-11-27.
First release.

## New
- Press a shortcut, speak, and the text lands in the app you were using; everything runs on your PC, nothing is uploaded
- Speech models that run on the CPU or an NVIDIA GPU, downloaded once and checked with SHA-256
- Several models can stay loaded in memory and load automatically; add your own model; hide the ones you do not use
- A model per language: pick a bigger model for the languages that need one
- Settings with a sidebar (Home, Models, Languages, Dictation, Overlay, App), a Home page that shows whether everything is ready, and a microphone level test
- Optional mic button inside the Windows taskbar (Settings > Overlay): click to dictate, point at it to open the pill, hold Ctrl and drag to move it; it stays where you put it
- Language indicator on the taskbar button (AUTO or the language code); right click it for a menu of languages that opens just above the taskbar and closes when you click elsewhere
- Accent colour (Settings > App): five accent colours (teal, blue, green, amber, slate) that recolor the tray icon, the taskbar button, the pill and the app icon; red for a missing microphone always stays red
- Tray menu: "Show dictation window" and a left click on the tray icon open the pill above the taskbar button and keep it there until you click another window, use its X or choose "Hide dictation window"
- Maximize and restore button in the settings title bar
- Microphone check: the tray icon and the taskbar button turn red when the microphone is unplugged, muted or blocked, and dictation shows a warning instead of starting
- Overlay with a small pill or a large card, bubble style, pin, opacity, sideways-only and lockable position, and a settings button
- Review the text before it is inserted, with a language menu, destination app name and inline hints in the pill
- Choose which languages the overlay menu lists
- Themed tooltips, light and dark themes, and an app icon in the settings title bar

## Install
- **Setup** (installs for all users into Program Files, asks for administrator rights): `Open-Local-Dictation-0.1.0-setup.exe`
- **Portable**: unzip `Open-Local-Dictation-0.1.0-portable.zip` anywhere and run `Open Local Dictation.exe`
