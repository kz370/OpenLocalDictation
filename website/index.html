Talk. It types. Nothing leaves your PC.

Three steps, no window to switch to

The pill sits above the taskbar and never steals the keyboard focus, so the app you are typing into stays exactly where it is.

Press the shortcut

Hold Ctrl+Alt+Space, or click the mic button in the taskbar. Nothing is listened to until then.

Speak

A live level meter and your words appear as you talk. The pill names the app the text will go into.

Review, then insert

Fix a word if you need to and press Ctrl+Enter. Or turn review off and the words are typed live.

New

A mic button inside the taskbar

An optional round button that lives next to your other taskbar widgets. The pill pops out of it.

  • Click to start dictating, click again to stop and insert.
  • Point at it and the pill opens above it. Move away and it closes.
  • Drag it anywhere along the taskbar. It stays exactly where you put it, whatever the tray does.
  • See the state at a glance: ready, listening, transcribing, review, inserted.
  • Red means trouble. If the microphone is unplugged, muted or blocked, the button and the tray icon turn red, and dictation tells you why instead of recording silence.

About 80 languages, one click away

Let Whisper detect the language, or pick one from the pill while you dictate. Switching reloads nothing, so it is instant.

  • Search the list, or keep only the languages you use in the menu.
  • Right-to-left languages such as Arabic are typed correctly, even under an English keyboard layout.
  • Changing the language mid-take restarts the take on the new language.

Small app, careful details

It does dictation and nothing else: no chat, no cloud, no account.

⚿

Runs on your PC

Whisper tiny, base or small on your CPU, or an NVIDIA GPU for speed. Downloads are verified with SHA-256.

✎

Review before inserting

On by default. Edit the text, then insert, retry or cancel with one key. Nothing reaches your document until you say so.

⌨

Types like you do

Real key events by scancode, so it is exact in Notepad and browsers. Or paste, restoring your clipboard afterwards.

⚑

Knows where the text goes

The pill shows the target app and hands the focus back to it before typing, even if you clicked away while talking.

☍

Share your models

Point it at a folder that already holds Whisper models so other apps can use the same files. It never deletes them.

⚙

Yours to arrange

Pill or bubble, pinned or on demand, any size, any opacity, light or dark. Or move it into the taskbar.

Everything is in Settings

A sidebar with six pages, one resizable window, and a light and a dark theme.

Private by construction

Dictation tools handle the most personal text you write. This one has nowhere to send it.

No cloud

Recognition runs on your machine. There is no account, no server and no telemetry.

No logs of what you say

Audio and dictated text are never written to disk or to the logs.

Verified downloads

Models come from pinned sources and are checked with SHA-256 before they are installed.

Hands off your files

The app never deletes models or the CUDA libraries. There is no delete button to press by accident.

Questions

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to download a model or the optional GPU libraries, once. Dictation itself works offline.

Which model should I pick?

Whisper small is the most accurate and is marked Recommended. Tiny and base are faster on older PCs. You can switch at any time.

Do I need an NVIDIA GPU?

No. The CPU works for everyday dictation. An NVIDIA GPU makes the larger model feel instant; the app downloads the CUDA libraries (about 1.7 GB) once when you choose it, or reuses a copy from Open Local Assistant.

Why does my taskbar button not show?

It needs the taskbar at the bottom of the screen. Settings > Overlay shows the reason when it cannot be placed. It is optional; the floating pill and bubble work anywhere.

The tray icon is red. What now?

The microphone is unplugged, muted or blocked by Windows' privacy setting. Hover the tray icon for the fix, or press the shortcut: the pill tells you what is wrong.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is open source under the GPL-3.0 license. The source, the specs and the build scripts are on GitHub.
